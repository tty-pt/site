# Seamless Shoreline & Continuous Water Architecture (SHORE.md)

## 1. Executive Summary & Core Invariants

The fundamental architectural invariant governing 2D continuous rendering in NeverDark is:
> **"Chunks can't disagree."**

If Chunk A evaluates coordinate $(wx, wy)$ at its boundary, and Chunk B evaluates the same coordinate $(wx, wy)$ at its adjacent boundary, both chunks must evaluate **identical depth, identical color, identical seabed substrate, and identical wave phase**. Any mathematical difference produces a visible rectangular chunk seam.

This document lays out the comprehensive diagnostic of all observed water edge artifacts and specifies the unified mathematical architecture for seamless, organic, beautiful water across NeverDark.

## 2. Diagnostic of Past Artifacts & Root Causes

### A. Artifact 1: Multi-Biome Coastline Corner Bites (Screenshot 030754)
- **Symptom**: When two different land biomes (e.g. Grassland and Shrubland) meet along a coastline, a massive triangular chunk of water is carved out of the land at their interface, meeting a flat vertical water wall with a depth cliff.
- **Root Cause**: In `ndw_ground_weights` (`external/nd-seven/include/nd/seven-world.h:815`), corner rounding evaluated `diff_x = 1` (land vs water) and `diff_y = 1` (land A vs land B). Because the diagonal neighbor was water, the code resolved `target_k = Water`, incorrectly assuming a land cape was jutting into the sea. In reality, land continued across the interface into the adjacent land biome.
- **Solution**: Enforce the cross-domain topological invariant: land bordering another land biome orthogonally may **never** round into water.

### B. Artifact 2: 1-Room Local Horizon & Depth-8 Clamp (Screenshots 093452 & 093706)
- **Symptom**: In rooms containing both land and water, the water renders as an unnatural rectangular block of light turquoise shallows that terminates along straight grid lines against deep navy ocean.
- **Root Cause**: 
  1. In `ground_blend_nbiomes` (`mods/nd/ux/nd_seven_ground.c:375`), encroaching water was hard-clamped with `if (d_units > 8) d_units = 8;`. All water inside a land chunk was forced to stay $\le 8$ (bright turquoise) regardless of distance from the beach.
  2. `render_water_cell` used a strictly local $3 \times 3$ neighbor stencil. Any water room whose immediate $\pm 1$ neighbors were water had `n_land == 0` and defaulted to abyss (depth 28), blind to land only 2 rooms away.
- **Solution**: Evaluate distance to the coastline continuously across the entire active $7 \times 7$ view window. Eliminate the artificial depth-8 clamp.

### C. Artifact 3: Coarse Bounding-Box Distance Disagreement (Screenshots 093706 & 100531)
- **Symptom**: In an L-shaped coastline (convex cape or concave bay), a flat rectangular shelf of shallow water appeared inside the land room, meeting ocean chunks with a straight vertical/horizontal seam.
- **Root Cause**: Treating land rooms as rigid $160 \times 128$ rectangular bounding boxes (`[x0, x0 + 160] × [y0, y0 + 128]`). Inside the land room, any encroaching water pixel was inside the room's box, evaluating distance as 0 (depth 1), while outside in the ocean chunk, distance was $> 0$.
- **Solution**: Distance must be measured to the **true organic coastline contour** generated by `ndw_ground_weights`, not coarse rectangular bounding boxes.

### D. Artifact 4: Diagonal Barber-Pole Surf Striping
- **Symptom**: Shoreline foam rendered as repetitive diagonal parallel bars (`/ / / / /`) at $45^\circ$ or $33^\circ$ along the coast.
- **Root Cause**: Modulo arithmetic on diagonal coordinate combinations `(gx * 2 + gy * 3 + drift) % N` or `(gx + gy) % 6`.
- **Solution**: Replace periodic slanted coordinate modulo with organic, multi-frequency noise caustics.

## 3. Mathematical Architecture: Continuous 7×7 Signed Distance Bathymetry

```
                     Continuous Depth Field D(wx, wy)
                     
   Highland / Turf    ▲
   ═══════════════════╬══════════════════════════════════════════ Shoreline (SDF = 0, D = 1)
   Beach Shallows     ║ ░░░░░░░░ Dynamic Caustic Ribbon (D = 1..2)
   ───────────────────╫────────────────────────────────────────── Shelf Break (D = 7..14)
   Continental Shelf  ║ ▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒ Luminous Aquamarine Shelf (D = 14..24)
   ───────────────────╫────────────────────────────────────────── Shelf Edge (D = 28)
   Abyssal Ocean      ║ ▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓ Deep Sapphire Ocean (D = 28)
                      ▼
```

### A. The 7×7 View Buffer as Global Context
The client receives the packed $7 \times 7$ room grid (`st->map_data`, 49 tiles). The viewport ($1280 \times 720$) displays roughly $4 \times 3$ rooms in the center. The outer ring of tiles is never visible on screen, acting as a complete seamless buffer.

### B. True Coastline Definition
Across the $7 \times 7$ grid, each tile $(c, r)$ is known to be Land (`bio_idx != 0`) or Water (`bio_idx == 0`).
The organic coastline is the continuous boundary where `ndw_ground_weights` transitions between land and water.
At any world coordinate $(wx, wy)$:
1. Sample the multi-octave noise warp $(ndx, ndy)$ via `ndw_border_warp`.
2. Compute the warped coordinate $(wx_{\text{warp}}, wy_{\text{warp}}) = (wx + ndx, wy + ndy)$.
3. Evaluate the signed distance to the nearest rounded land tile contour across the entire $7 \times 7$ view window.

### C. Unified Depth Equation
Depth across **all chunks** (water chunks, ocean basins, and encroaching water in land chunks) is governed by one identical function:
$$\text{dist} = \text{distance\_to\_coast}(wx_{\text{warp}}, wy_{\text{warp}})$$

$$\text{depth}(wx, wy) = \begin{cases}
1 + \left\lfloor \frac{27 \cdot \text{dist}}{64} \right\rfloor & \text{if } \text{dist} < 64 \\
28 & \text{if } \text{dist} \ge 64
\end{cases}$$

- At the beach ($\text{dist} = 0$): $\text{depth} = 1$.
- Shallows ($0 < \text{dist} \le 16$): $\text{depth} = 1 \dots 7$.
- Continental Shelf ($16 < \text{dist} \le 48$): $\text{depth} = 7 \dots 21$.
- Deep Ocean ($\text{dist} \ge 64$): $\text{depth} = 28$.

Because $(wx, wy)$ are absolute world coordinates, **Chunk A and Chunk B evaluate the exact same depth byte down to the pixel**.

## 4. Visual Shading & Optical Caustics

### A. Volumetric Optical Extinction (Sub-Linear Attenuation)
Water color and opacity transition gradually over the extended 64-unit shelf:
- **Depth 1–6 (Shallows)**: Luminous tropical cerulean shallows (`RGB(64, 180, 224)`), Alpha $80 \dots 125$. The sandy seabed is clearly visible.
- **Depth 6–18 (Shelf)**: Coastal shelf azure (`RGB(42, 136, 205)`), Alpha $125 \dots 180$.
- **Depth 18–28 (Deep Basin)**: Deep sapphire ocean (`RGB(18, 52, 130)`), Alpha $180 \dots 235$.

### B. Dynamic Caustic Shoreline Shimmer
In shallow water ($\text{depth} \le 2$), simulate sunlight refraction dancing on the seabed:
```c
uint32_t drift = anim / 4;
int c1 = (int)(unit_noise(0x43415553u, (gx + drift) / 2, (gy + drift / 2) / 2) & 31);
int c2 = (int)(unit_noise(0x54494353u, (gx - drift / 2) / 2, (gy + drift) / 2) & 31);
int caustic = c1 + c2; /* 0..62 interfering light web */

if (d == 1) {
    int foam_alpha = 80 + (caustic > 38 ? (caustic - 38) * 3 : 0);
    uint32_t light_col = (caustic > 42) ? RGB(248, 255, 254) : RGB(225, 248, 252);
    col = alpha_blend_rgb(col, light_col, foam_alpha);
} else if (d == 2 && caustic > 36) {
    int c_alpha = (caustic - 36) * 3;
    col = alpha_blend_rgb(col, RGB(210, 245, 252), c_alpha);
}
```
Zero diagonal banding, zero barber-pole striping. Light ripples naturally along the organic contour of the sand.

### C. Consistent Seabed Substrate
In deep water ($\text{dist} \ge 24$ units), the seabed under the water is uniform sandy seabed (`NDW_G_SAND`). Only in immediate beach shallows ($\text{dist} < 24$ units) does the seabed harmonize with the local land biome (snow, ash, tundra, desert). This guarantees 100% color consistency across ocean room boundaries.
